About OpenHotel

OpenHotel is a chill-out space where you can hang out with your friends. Guests are represented in the hotel by a little pixel person you design yourself. Anyone can check in.

Go on a tour of the hotel >>

Run your own hotel

OpenHotel is open source. Download the server and you can open a hotel for your friends, your school or your whole town.
